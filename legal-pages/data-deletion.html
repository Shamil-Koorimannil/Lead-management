User Data Deletion Instructions

Application: Lead Management | Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

The Lead Management application respects your privacy and provides users with a clear process to request the deletion of any personal data associated with their Instagram interactions or business enquiries.

2. Scope of Data Deletion

Upon receiving a verified deletion request, we can remove stored records associated with your profile, which may include:

  • Instagram user identifiers and account references.
  • Instagram message history, message IDs, and interaction timestamps.
  • Franchise enquiry details, contact notes, and qualification status records.
  • Assigned sales follow-up notes and contact logs.

3. How to Submit a Request

To request data deletion, please contact the application administrator responsible for Lead Management.

To help us locate and accurately identify your records for deletion, please provide the following details in your request:

  • Your Instagram username or profile handle used during the interaction.
  • The approximate date and time of your enquiry or messaging interaction.
  • Any relevant context or details submitted during your inquiry.

4. Processing & Legal Exceptions

Once a request is received and verified, your data will be permanently removed from our active database systems.

Please note that applicable personal information may be retained where necessary to satisfy legitimate legal obligations, regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, or security retention requirements.

5. Contact Information

For data deletion inquiries or assistance, please reach out directly to the Lead Management application administrator.
