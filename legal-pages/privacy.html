Privacy Policy

Application: Lead Management | Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Lead Management application ("the Application") receives, processes, stores, and manages personal information collected through its Instagram messaging integration and related lead-management functionality.

2. Information We Process

When users interact with Lead Management via messaging channels or franchise enquiries, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Instagram User Identifiers: Technical account and user identifiers provided via Meta/Instagram APIs.
  • Username & Display Information: Public Instagram username and profile display name where made available by the platform.
  • Instagram Message Content: Text and media content contained within messages sent to our connected business profile.
  • Message Identifiers & Timestamps: Technical message IDs, conversation reference codes, and timestamps of interaction.
  • Franchise Enquiry Information: Contact details, location preferences, and business background provided during franchise or service inquiries.
  • Lead Qualification Information: Qualification status, criteria scores, notes, and records produced during inquiry evaluation.
  • Follow-up Information: Communication history, scheduled tasks, operational notes, and sales interaction logs.

3. How Information Is Used

The information collected is strictly used for operational and business enquiry management purposes, including:

  • Receiving, processing, and responding to incoming user enquiries.
  • Qualifying franchise and business leads.
  • Communicating directly with inquiring users regarding their inquiries.
  • Assigning qualified leads to designated sales and administrative personnel.
  • Scheduling follow-up communications and maintaining accurate operational records.

4. Data Storage & Access Control

All processed information is stored within controlled and secure database environments. Access to personal data and conversation logs is strictly restricted to authorized personnel responsible for lead management, sales follow-up, and system administration.

5. No Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or commercialize personal information or message data to third parties under any circumstances.

6. Data Retention

Personal information and conversation records are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes set forth in this policy, complete requested follow-ups, or comply with applicable legal, security, and record-keeping requirements.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users have the right to request deletion of data associated with their Instagram interaction or franchise enquiry. For details on how to submit a request, please visit our Data Deletion Instructions page.

8. Third-Party Platform Relationship

Lead Management integrates with services provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (Instagram). Interaction with Instagram remains subject to Meta's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Lead Management functions as an independent integration utilizing official Meta APIs.

9. Contact / Privacy Requests

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or personal data management, please contact the administrator responsible for the Lead Management application.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect operational, legal, or technical changes. Any revisions will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
